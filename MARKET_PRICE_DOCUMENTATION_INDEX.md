## 💡 Key Concepts

### Forecasting Formula
```
Forecast Price = Current Price × (1 + Total Adjustment %)

Total Adjustment = Climate + Rainfall + Soil + Disease
```

### Adjustment Ranges
- Climate Risk: 0-8%
- Rainfall Impact: -3% to +5%
- Soil Quality: -2% to +4%
- Disease Risk: 0-6%

### Supported Crops
1. Rice
2. Wheat
3. Maize
4. Cotton
5. Tomato

### Color Coding
- Green: Price increase (↑)
- Red: Price decrease (↓)
- Gray: No change (→)
